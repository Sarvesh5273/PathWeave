# Interest exploration and funding — locked product direction

Confirmed by Sarvesh on October 17, 2026, IST. He subsequently authorized implementation.
The first exploration/strict-filter slice is implemented and verified in v0.5.0;
see flow-implementation.md. Timetable, readiness and learning-roadmap design remains
separate pending work. The initial discussion-only lock is preserved below as intent.

## Interest exploration

- Use approximately 10–12 beginner-friendly project scenarios drawn from company-role
  work and research-internship work, rather than only broad activity checkboxes.
- Explain the project/problem, what an intern would do, and the activities involved.
  Ask which activities the student would like to try; no prior technical knowledge
  or correct answer is required. Include haven't tried, several, none and skip options.
- Use several observations per direction. Interest is tentative and distinct from
  demonstrated skill or readiness. Do not claim a validated assessment or career prediction.
- Recommend 2–3 directions with reasons tied to answers and small activities to try.
  Users can edit interests, retake exploration and change their search keywords.
- Cover company and research contexts without treating them as mutually exclusive
  interests. The same student may enjoy both software building and research.
- Real projects need public source attribution. Authored composite scenarios must be
  identified as illustrative, not presented as a particular company's real assignment.
- Scenario wording, scoring/mapping and usability still need design/review. This lock
  does not establish validity or authorize broader implementation before discussion.

Illustrative examples for discussion:

Company: a shopping app loses orders. Explore building a fix, investigating usage data,
or improving how users understand checkout. Explain each activity in ordinary language.

Research: a robot misses obstacles in dim light. Explore comparing observations,
testing possible explanations, or building and measuring an improvement. Explain that
research involves uncertain answers and iteration; it can also involve writing software.

## Funding

Visible options: Any funding; Paid/stipend; Stipend + accommodation + round-trip travel.
The strict third option requires explicit source evidence for all three components.
Show amounts, duration, limits, reimbursement/upfront payment, fees and uncovered costs.
Do not imply a stipend is sufficient for all living expenses or that all costs are zero.

Unknown coverage is not a confirmed match. Exclude unknown/mismatching coverage from
strict results; offer an explicit optional view of unconfirmed coverage. Do not silently
relax selected requirements. Use the concrete component label rather than relying on
the phrase fully funded. Earlier Scout meaning (stipend plus travel OR housing) is
superseded for this product filter.

## Still to discuss

Required topic checks versus a beginner start-from-basics route; editable timetable
uploads and exam buffers; sourced interview stages; shared topic/prerequisite/resource
roadmaps and permitted content reuse. No decision on these is implied by this lock.
Strict location filtering and always-visible fine-tuning remain requested improvements,
not implemented capabilities. Current app is not claimed to fulfill the full vision.
