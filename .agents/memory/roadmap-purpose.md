---
name: Roadmap purpose
description: User's intended value and hierarchy for the preparation roadmap.
---

The roadmap's primary job is to show a topic-wise sequence of what to study for
interview preparation. A requirements-verification checklist is not a substitute.

**Why:** On October 18, 2026, the user reviewed an actual laptop-generated plan and
could not find a topic-wise study plan among the warnings, checks and verification
tasks. They explicitly identified information overload as a product problem.

**How to apply:** Evaluate roadmap changes by whether users can immediately find
their next study topic and understand the sequence. Keep application verification
distinct from learning work. Distinguish recommended curriculum from confirmed
employer requirements rather than inventing evidence for missing source details.
