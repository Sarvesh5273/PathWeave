---
name: App chrome widths
description: Separate navbar/footer layout from readable form-column widths.
---

Keep the navbar and footer full-width with modest inner gutters. Main content and
forms may remain narrower and centered.

**Why:** On October 18, 2026, the user rejected the excessive laptop side margins
created by putting navbar, footer and main content inside one narrow shared frame.
This supersedes earlier shared-frame directions in the historical decision log.

**How to apply:** Treat app chrome and form-column widths as separate constraints;
do not widen all forms merely to fix header/footer spacing.
