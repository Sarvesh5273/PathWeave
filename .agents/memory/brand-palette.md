---
name: Brand palette scope
description: The established PathWeave palette remains a constraint during redesigns.
---

Keep PathWeave's cream/orange palette. Do not introduce green, blue, or pale cool
panels as decorative secondary branding without a specific request.

**Why:** On October 18, 2026, the user authorized a full responsive redesign with
animations but then explicitly rejected the introduced green and pale cool panels.
Broad redesign authorization did not mean a brand-palette change.

**How to apply:** Improve layout, type, hierarchy, responsiveness and motion within
the existing palette. Treat a palette change as a separate decision.
